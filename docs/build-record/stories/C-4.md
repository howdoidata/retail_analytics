## Story spec

**C-4: Build the source CSV generator (Ch1)** (CAP-5)

> Python generator that writes the contract's six CSVs with mess M1–M6 at the contract volume (2+ years,
> 10,000+ customers, ~1.65 visits per customer per quarter), deterministic from a seed, with Python tests (CAP-5).

Decisions made with Antonio before implementation: timestamps without an offset are UTC (added to
[data-contracts.md](../../data-contracts.md)); the acquisition channel stays first touch at or before signup,
and the touch data also supports other attribution rules later; "2+ years" means the 8 full quarters
2024-Q1 to 2025-Q4.
