## Status

**In progress.** Done: sample extracts of the three marts and the dashboard layout. Not done: the workbook
is not authored yet (Antonio authors it in Tableau; it is not published until C-13).

Data-contract questions found while preparing the samples, now answered in
[data-contracts.md](../../data-contracts.md). Antonio answered 1 and 2; 3 to 7 take the recommended answer,
the one the samples already follow, at his direction (keep transformation minimal, get the pipeline working
end to end first), to be revisited once the dashboard is visible. The samples needed no regeneration.

1. Funnel grain: stays monthly (cohort month). The dashboard rolls months up to quarters by summing counts,
   recomputing rates, and weighting average days by customers reached.
2. `signups_prior_quarter` for the first quarter: null (no earlier data), not 0.
3. `cost_per_signup`: null when sign-ups or ad spend is 0; channels with no spend carry ad spend 0.
4. Funnel signup row (milestone 0): `previous_milestone`, `step_rate`, and `avg_days_from_previous` are null;
   ad spend appears only on that row.
5. Recent cohorts: the marts keep every cohort with no flag; the dashboard filters them by default.
6. "1.65 visits per customer per quarter" means per active customer (`visits_per_active_customer`).
7. Export formats are pinned in the contract: ISO dates, `true`/`false`, empty cell for null, and set
   rounding per column.
