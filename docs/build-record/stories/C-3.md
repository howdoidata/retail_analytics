Open data-contract questions, found while preparing the samples. They are answered in the spec before the
models are built (C-4 to C-9):

1. The funnel mart's grain is the cohort month, while sign-ups and cohorts are quarterly. Keep the funnel
   monthly or align it to the quarter?
2. `signups_prior_quarter` for the first quarter: null (sample default) or 0?
3. `cost_per_signup`: null when sign-ups are 0, when ad spend is 0, or both? (Sample: null if either is 0;
   organic channels have no spend.)
4. The funnel's signup row (milestone 0): `step_rate` and `avg_days_from_previous` null (sample default) or
   1 and 0? Ad spend appears only on the signup row.
5. Immature cohorts (too young to reach later milestones): handle them in the mart (a flag or age column) or
   only filter in the dashboard? (The sample layout hides cohorts under 6 months.)
6. Does "1.65 visits per customer per quarter" mean per active customer (sample assumption) or across all
   customers?
7. Export value formats: ISO dates (YYYY-MM-DD), booleans as true/false, an empty cell for null, rates
   rounded to 6 decimals (sample assumption). Pin these in the contract?
